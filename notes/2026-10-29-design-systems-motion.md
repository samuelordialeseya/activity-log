# Engineering Notes: Fluid Spring Physics & Glassmorphic Optics

**Date:** October 29, 2026  
**Category:** UI / UX Engineering / Motion Design  

## Overview
Fine-tuning gesture spring physics, tokenized blur filters, and liquid glassmorphic navigation components.

### Core Principles
1. **Physical Spring Tuning**:
   - Using mass, damping, and stiffness ratios that prevent bounce overshoot on critical navigation actions.
   - Targeting damping ratio ~0.82 for responsive deceleration without sluggish settle times.
2. **Layer Separation & Optical Clarity**:
   - Combining backdrop filter blur with subtle 1px border highlights to maintain contrast over dynamic backgrounds.
